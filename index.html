Pravo dlya detey — Law for Kids

Pravo dlya detey (Law for Kids) is an educational project: short animated videos in which cartoon characters explain the laws of the Republic of Belarus to children in a simple and funny way.

The Pravo dlya detey Uploader is an internal tool used only by the project owner to publish the project's own videos to the project's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.
