Pravo dlya detey Uploader — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026.

1. The App is used only by the owner of the Pravo dlya detey project for the owner's own accounts. It does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person.

2. The App receives from TikTok and Google only the access tokens and the account identifier of the project owner, needed to upload videos. Tokens are stored encrypted in a private repository and are never shared with third parties.

3. The App does not use cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking.

4. The App does not collect any data from viewers of the videos, including children.

5. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings; the stored tokens then become invalid and are deleted.
